HoopDream Uploader

HoopDream Uploader is a private tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel @theHoopDream to upload and schedule short-form videos to that channel through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public, and it only acts on the channel of the person who signs in.

What it does:

  • Uploads finished videos with their title, description, tags and category.
  • Schedules videos to go public at a chosen time.
  • Checks the processing and privacy status of videos it uploaded.
