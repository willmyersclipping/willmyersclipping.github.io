Clips

Clips is a publishing tool run by Will Myers. It posts short vertical videos that our team edits from podcasts and shows to the accounts we own and manage: theHoopDream and Wildcard Cuts.

How it works

  • Our team edits each clip and a person reviews and approves it.
  • Each of our accounts signs in once through TikTok's own login.
  • The app uploads the approved clip to that account with the privacy and interaction settings we choose for the post.

The app is for our own team only. It is not open to the public.
