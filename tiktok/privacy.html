Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

Clips ("the app") is a publishing tool run by Will Myers. It posts short videos that our team edits to TikTok accounts we own and manage: theHoopDream and Wildcard Cuts. The app is not offered to the public.

What we collect from TikTok

When one of our accounts signs in to the app through TikTok, we receive:

  • Basic profile information: the account's open ID, display name and profile picture.
  • Access and refresh tokens that let the app post to that account.
  • Posting settings TikTok returns before each post, such as the privacy options and whether comments, duets and stitches are allowed.

How we use it

  • To confirm which of our accounts a video will be posted to.
  • To upload and publish videos our team has reviewed and approved.
  • To check whether a post finished processing.

We do not use this information for advertising, profiling or anything other than posting to our own accounts.

Where it is stored

Tokens and profile details are stored only on our own computer, in files that are not shared or published. We do not sell, rent or share this information with anyone.

How long we keep it

We keep tokens while an account is connected. When an account is disconnected, we delete its tokens. You can also revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok's settings under security and permissions.
